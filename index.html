About

I am an undergraduate law student at the Department of Law, University of Rajshahi, pursuing an LL.B. (Hons.) with a strong academic and professional interest in legal research, human rights, and social justice. My academic training has developed my understanding of legal principles, research methodology, and the practical application of law to contemporary social issues. Through academic research, fieldwork, and engagement in university-based activities, I have sought to strengthen my analytical, communication, and advocacy skills.
